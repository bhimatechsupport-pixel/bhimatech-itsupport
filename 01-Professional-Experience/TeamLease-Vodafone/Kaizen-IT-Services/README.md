## 4. Typical IT Support Workflow

My general approach to managing IT support activities:

1. Receive and understand the employee's technical issue.
2. Identify the affected device, application, or service.
3. Perform initial diagnosis and troubleshooting.
4. Implement the appropriate resolution.
5. Verify functionality with the employee.
6. Update the relevant IT records.
7. Escalate issues requiring additional technical expertise.
8. Follow up to ensure the issue is resolved.
