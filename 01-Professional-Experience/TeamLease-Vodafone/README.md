## 4. Typical Troubleshooting Workflow

My general approach to resolving user issues:

1. Understand the user's problem and collect relevant information.
2. Identify the affected device, application, or service.
3. Perform initial troubleshooting and identify the possible cause.
4. Apply an appropriate resolution within assigned access and permissions.
5. Verify the issue is resolved with the end user.
6. Update the incident or service request with troubleshooting details.
7. Escalate unresolved issues to the appropriate technical team.
8. Follow up and close the ticket according to the support process.
